## 1. Immutable North Star

Build OASIS WEDDINGS into the most trusted, visible, operationally reliable and commercially effective wedding photography + cinematography company originating in Murcia, then scale through Levante, Spain and destination weddings.

The company must win because of real evidence: excellent portfolio, client experience, venue/planner relationships, authority, fast sales operations, reliable production, measurable acquisition and profitable unit economics.

## 3. Anti-loop law

A new subsystem is allowed only if it:

1. removes a demonstrated bottleneck;
2. protects a hard invariant;
3. reduces repeated operating cost;
4. improves acquisition/conversion/delivery/authority; or
5. enables a blocked goal that cannot be solved more simply.

Three consecutive waves with no external business output trigger `ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE`.
